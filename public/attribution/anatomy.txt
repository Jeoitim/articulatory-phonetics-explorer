This site's interactive anatomy is a simplified, redrawn pedagogical SVG with separate tongue, jaw, lip, velum and airflow layers. It is not a medical image or a subject-specific reconstruction.
The interactive anatomical SVG adaptation is offered under CC BY-SA 3.0; changes include redrawing, recoloring, Chinese/English labels and dynamic articulators.

The original static contours are retained in their native coordinate system. Movable tongue, velum, lip and mandibular surfaces replace the corresponding original structures. Bone texture, nasal conchae, muscle fibers and laryngeal inlet overlays are qualitative educational additions, not a measured anatomical reconstruction.

MRI and contour literature (bibliographic references, not incorporated assets)

Zainab Hermes, Marissa Barlaz, Ryan Shosted, Zhi-Pei Liang, Brad Sutton (2017).
Phonetic Correlates of Pharyngeal and Pharyngealized Consonants in Saudi, Lebanese, and Jordanian Arabic: an rt-MRI Study.
Interspeech 2017, pp. 201-205. DOI: 10.21437/Interspeech.2017-1601.
https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2017/hermes17_interspeech.html
https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2017/hermes17_interspeech.pdf#page=3
Especially PDF page 3 (printed p. 203), Figures 1-6: MRI boundary extraction, pharyngeal /ħ ʕ/ and pharyngealized /sˤ/ contours across three speakers. Reference for a continuous posterior tongue contour and variation in constriction location; not a universal template or a fitted reconstruction.

Saeed Shar and John Ingram (2011). MRI Investigation of Arabic Gutturals.
Proceedings of ICPhS XVII, pp. 1802-1805.
https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/icphs-proceedings/ICPhS2011/OnlineProceedings/RegularSession/Shar/Shar.pdf#page=2
Especially PDF page 2 (printed p. 1803), Figure 1: MRI measurement of tongue-root/pharyngeal width, epiglottal width and laryngeal height. These are distinct anatomical measures, not a single root displacement.
